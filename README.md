- 🏦 **Oakpoint Advisors, Data Integrity Specialist (Co-op).** I built and deployed a Python pipeline that connects **LLMs to the DealCloud API**. It extracts investor mandates, fuzzy-matches contacts and updates the CRM by rule, with source citations and confidence checks for human review. I also benchmark credit strategies on alpha, beta, tracking error and capture ratios.
- 📈 **E[X] Trading, Director of Research.** I lead research for a **100+ member prediction-market desk**. I replay recorded order flow under different liquidity settings to compare market-making policies on loss limits and manipulation resistance.
- 🧪 **Research Assistant, Northeastern.** I coauthored a working paper on **holding rewards & participation in Polymarket**. Our poster was accepted at the **12th Annual Bloomberg–Columbia Machine Learning in Finance Conference (2026)**. I also audited causal and ML analyses across **236 markets**, checking for data leakage and imbalanced comparison groups.
